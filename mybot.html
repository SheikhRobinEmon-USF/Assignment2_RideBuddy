🎡 Your family ride finder

Ride Buddy

Find the rides your child can go on, before you get to the park.

🎢

Ride Buddy is an AI assistant built with Botpress Studio. Parents can type their child’s height in inches or centimeters, and the bot checks it against each ride’s minimum height to help find a great day-out fit.

Easy as 1, 2, 3

How it works

A quick height check helps you plan the fun before you arrive.

1

Open the chat

Tap the chat bubble in the bottom right corner to start talking with Ride Buddy.

2

Share your plan

Type your child’s height and tell the bot which park you’re visiting.

3

Find your rides

Get a helpful list of rides your child is tall enough to ride.
